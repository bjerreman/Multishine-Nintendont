# Credits

Multishine for Wii is made by Nigzel (Bjerreman).

It stands on a lot of other people's work:

- **Nintendont.** This loader is a modified build of Nintendont by FIX94, crediar and the Nintendont contributors, the program that runs GameCube games on a Wii and a Wii U. Nintendont is licensed under GPL-2.0, and so is this loader as built. Thank you to everyone who made it. Upstream: https://github.com/FIX94/Nintendont
- **Slippi.** Online Melee on real hardware exists because the Slippi team spent years on rollback netcode, matchmaking and the replay format. Multishine follows their protocol, and without them it would never have existed. Please support Slippi: slippi.gg.
- **The Melee modding community.** The memory card exploit that starts Multishine was found by wParam in 2008 and developed through the 20XX Training Hack Pack, 20XX Tournament Edition, UCF and UnclePunch's Training Mode. Thank you to everyone who kept that work going.
- **libogc, libogc2 and devkitPro.** The loader is built with devkitPPC and devkitARM and links libogc. Our network adapter code follows the behaviour of the libogc and libogc2 drivers. Thank you to the devkitPro, libogc and libogc2 contributors. Their licence notices are in `NOTICE` and `LICENSES/`.
- **The Wii homebrew community.** The Homebrew Channel starts the loader, and the WiiBrew documentation of IOS, the network and the storage devices made the network adapter possible.
- **Aileron.** The text on the loader's screens is set in Aileron by Sora Sagano, released as CC0.
- **GTS Root R1 to R4.** The public root certificates of Google Trust Services are built in to check the update server.

Multishine is not affiliated with Nintendo, Slippi or the Nintendont project. No game data is included in this repository: you bring your own copy of Melee.
